Recommendations to reduce adversary attacks

  • Use parameterized database queries and validate input on the server.
  • Prevent XSS with textContent, contextual output encoding, safe HTML sanitization, and a strict Content Security Policy.
  • Use HTTPS, secure session cookies (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite), CSRF protection, and multi-factor authentication.
  • Keep dependencies and frameworks patched; remove exposed secrets and rotate leaked credentials.
  • Apply least privilege, rate limits, logging/monitoring, backups, and tested incident-response procedures.
  • Test only systems you own or have explicit permission to assess.

These are general defensive practices, not a guarantee against every attack.
